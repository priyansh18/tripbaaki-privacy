The service

TripBaaki is a free, ad-free app for tracking and settling a group trip's shared expenses. We provide it as is, and we may change or discontinue features. TripBaaki never processes payments or holds money -- it only opens your own UPI app with the amount pre-filled; the payment itself is between you and the UPI network.

Your content

Trip names, member names, expenses and settlement records you enter belong to you. You give us permission to store and display them to anyone you share the trip's link with. Do not enter content that is illegal, hateful, harassing, or that you do not have the right to share.

Trip links are a shared secret, not a login

A trip's 6-character code lets anyone holding it view the trip and mark a payment as paid, with no account or password. Share it only with people in that trip, the same way you would a shared document link. We are not responsible for a trip link shared with the wrong person.

Payment accuracy is self-reported

"I've paid" is a self-report by whoever taps it; the organizer confirming it is what actually closes a settlement in the app. TripBaaki does not verify that a UPI payment actually happened -- always confirm with the other person directly for anything that matters.

Deleting your data

The organizer can delete their account from Settings at any time, which deletes every trip they created for everyone who held its link.
