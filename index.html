TripBaaki Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026. TripBaaki is published by AppLab Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the TripBaaki Android app, and the no-install web link it generates, collect, why, and how you can delete it.

What TripBaaki is

TripBaaki helps a group settle a shared trip's expenses. One person (the organizer) installs the app, creates a trip and adds the names of everyone splitting it. Everyone else gets a link -- no app, no sign-up -- to see what they owe, pay with their own UPI app, and mark it paid. TripBaaki never processes payments itself; it only opens your UPI app with the amount pre-filled.

Data we collect

  • Anonymous account ID (organizer only). On first launch of the app, we create a random account ID. No email, phone number or password is required. We do not know who the organizer is.
  • Trip data. The trip name, the names of the people in it (as typed by the organizer, who may or may not be their real names), expenses, amounts, and settlement records. This is visible to the organizer and to anyone the organizer shares the trip's link with.
  • UPI ID (optional). If the organizer or a member adds a UPI ID (a payment address like name@bank), it is used only to pre-fill a UPI payment link. We never see or store banking credentials, card numbers or OTPs -- those stay entirely inside your UPI app.
  • The trip link / code. A 6-character code is the only thing that lets someone view or act on a trip without an account. Anyone with the link can view the trip's balances and mark a payment as paid; treat it like you would any shared document link.
  • Usage events. A small set of product events (for example "expense added", "payment marked paid") with timestamps, tied to the organizer's anonymous account ID. They never contain names, amounts or the trip code.
  • Crash reports. Device model, OS version and the technical error only, never trip content or the trip code.

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, files, advertising ID or device identifiers. The app requests no Android permissions.

Where data is stored

Data is stored on Supabase (supabase.com) servers in the Mumbai, India region, protected by row-level security. Data travels over HTTPS only. The organizer's session token is kept in their phone's encrypted storage; guests using the link never get a session at all.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers. Supabase processes it as our hosting provider. Tapping "Pay via UPI" opens your own UPI app with the amount pre-filled; that app's own privacy policy applies to the payment itself, which TripBaaki never sees. Sharing a trip link sends it through whichever app you choose (e.g. WhatsApp); that app's privacy policy applies to that message.

Deleting your data

  • Delete account (Settings, organizer only) deletes every trip the organizer created, its members, expenses and payment records, immediately and permanently. Anyone holding that trip's link can no longer open it afterwards.

Because the organizer's account is anonymous, we cannot restore it once deleted. Guests never create an account, so there is nothing of theirs to separately delete; removing a trip (via the organizer deleting their account) removes everything in it.

Children

TripBaaki is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
